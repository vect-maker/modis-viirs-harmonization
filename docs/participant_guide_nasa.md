## DOCUMENTATION

### PDF Structure (1 of 2)

The proposal is organized into eight sections that form part of a single document, prepared in both Spanish and English.

* **One document, two languages (`ES` / `EN`):** All eight sections are included in both versions, complete with screenshots, diagrams, and links where applicable.

#### PROPOSAL DOCUMENT (8 SECTIONS IN TOTAL) — PART 1/2

1. **Project Identification:** Name of the proposal and the team; team members, chosen official challenge, and a clear identification of the presented solution.

2. **Proposal Summary:** Brief explanation of what the team developed, what problem it addresses, how it responds to the challenge, and its main outcome.

3. **Problem and Objectives:** Description of the context, need, or question motivating the project. Specify the general objective and desired outcomes.

4. **Developed Solution:** Explain the proposal, how it works or its approach, its components, and key decisions. Highlight the contribution that sets it apart.

### PDF Structure (2 of 2)

Continuation of the same document: sections 5 through 8 describe the development, evidence, and results.

#### PROPOSAL DOCUMENT (8 SECTIONS IN TOTAL) — PART 2/2

5. **Methodology and Scientific Resources:** Describe the work process, methodological decisions, and data or resources from NASA and partner agencies. Explain how they were applied to the solution.

6. **Development, Results, and Evidence:** Present the obtained results through screenshots, charts, tables, photographs, diagrams, simulations, models, or links to demos, as appropriate.

7. **Impact and Projection:** Identify beneficiaries, potential applications, scope, and usefulness of the solution. Outline opportunities for improvement or future development.

8. **References and Tools Used:** Identify scientific sources and datasets used; include links and mention relevant tools, including the use of artificial intelligence where applicable.

> **How to present evidence:** Graphic elements can be embedded directly within the PDF. For interactive prototypes, files, or full productions, public links may be added.
